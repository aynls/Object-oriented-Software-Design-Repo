# 场景插图

页面使用 `slime-worlds-cutout.png` 与 `slime-cover-cutout.png` 两个透明版本。原始生成版本保留用于后续调整。

透明版本均由内置 image_gen 编辑得到。地图编辑提示词：Remove only the off-white background outside the three terrain dioramas, replacing it with genuine transparency. Preserve the three dioramas, every slime, rocks, greenery, water, their exact order, size, colors and positions. Preserve the wide horizontal composition and full uncropped subjects. No text, labels, frames or additional objects.

封面编辑提示词：Remove only the off-white backdrop outside the green slime and its small grass-and-stone patch. Replace the backdrop with genuine transparency. Preserve the exact slime identity, face, pose, color, translucent body, sprout, grass and stones, their original scale and right-side placement, and the same landscape canvas. No cropping, no text, no labels, no other additions.

使用内置 image_gen 工具生成，图片已复制到项目 public/images，页面不依赖外部图片地址。

## slime-worlds.png

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide illustration for a Chinese university presentation explaining distributed game configuration. Generate a polished wide horizontal 3:1 composition: three clearly separated miniature game environments arranged left to right, a lush green forest clearing, a turquoise ocean coast with a tiny sandy island, and high cool gray alpine mountains with sparse grass. One small friendly translucent green slime creature, same creature design and same size, in each environment. Refined stylized 3D game concept art, soft natural daylight, matte miniature diorama materials, playful but restrained, clear silhouettes and generous breathing room. Each terrain is a distinct small island on an uninterrupted solid warm off-white background #f6f5f2. Camera elevated isometric view. No frame, no panels, no letters, no text, no numbers, no labels, no arrows, no diagram, no UI, no watermark. Keep the environments in a horizontal band with clean margins; nothing cropped.
